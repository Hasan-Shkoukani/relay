How an X-ray gets read

  1. The dental model goes first. It marks fillings, implants, cavities and impacted teeth, and answers come from its findings.
  2. GPT vision looks next, only when the model has no answer: for photos that aren’t X-rays, or questions the findings don’t cover.
  3. General knowledge is the last resort, and it’s labelled, so you always know it didn’t come from your image.

How far to trust the findings

We downloaded eleven pretrained dental models, scored the seven that look for these findings on 194 panoramic X-rays none of them had seen, and kept the most accurate. Each bar shows how reliably it says whether a finding is in an image (F1 score, where 1.00 is perfect).

  • Fillings0.93
  • Cavity0.56
  • Impacted tooth0.50
  • Implant0.39

Fillings are dependable. Treat cavities, impacted teeth and implants as a second look, not a verdict. Relay gives general information, not a diagnosis, so have a dentist confirm anything that matters.
